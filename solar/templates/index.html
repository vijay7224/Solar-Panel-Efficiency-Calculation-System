Quick Insights

  • Temperature impact: Most panels lose ~0.3–0.4% power per °C above 25°C (check your panel's temperature coefficient – lower is better, e.g. -0.27%/°C for premium models).
  • Real-world vs STC: Efficiency shown here is usually relative to real conditions (not STC 1000 W/m², 25°C). High module temp reduces output significantly in hot climates like Madhya Pradesh summers.
  • Need panel area or STC rating?: For the most accurate % efficiency, divide DC power by (Irradiance × Panel Area). If your backend uses a model/formula, consider adding system capacity (kWp) as an input.
  • Typical degradation: Modern panels lose only ~0.4–0.6% per year – still >80–90% after 25 years.
